## Who this is

Chef, founder of Momofuku restaurant group, TV personality (Ugly Delicious, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner), podcast host (The Dave Chang Show). One of the few hospitality figures who built genuine cross-platform media presence before anyone had a name for it. The distribution and monetization arc is the study — how a chef becomes a media entity.
