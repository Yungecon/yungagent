## Overview

In October 2025, David Chang opened Super Peach by Momofuku at Westfield Century City mall in Los Angeles — a new restaurant concept that represents Momofuku's return to full-service restaurant operation after the 2023 closures of Ko and Ssäm Bar.

## Concept

Super Peach celebrates "American dining through the Momofuku lens with a collaborative, playful menu from David Chang and the Momofuku Culinary team that reflects the vibrant food cultures of Los Angeles, and beyond."

The concept is Chang's team's take on "American classics, reimagined" — blending Korean, Californian, and American comfort food with Momofuku's signature swagger.

## Location Strategy: The Mall Bet

**Location**: Westfield Century City, Level 1, near the Santa Monica Boulevard valet entrance — a major upscale shopping mall in Los Angeles

**Why a mall?** This is a significant strategic signal:
- Mall anchor tenants are increasingly restaurants, not retail stores (Chang himself said in 2018: "Restaurants are replacing retail as anchor tenants, and everyone wants one")
- Century City specifically: Affluent West Los Angeles demographics, heavy foot traffic, entertainment complex adjacency
- CNBC had previously quoted Chang predicting this trend; Super Peach is its execution

## Space Details

- Design firm: INC Architecture and Design (same firm that worked on Momofuku's Noodle Bar)
- Capacity: 196 indoor seats
- Outdoor patio: 24 seats
- Design aesthetic: Floor-to-ceiling green hues with Momofuku's signature saturated orange accents

## Menu

All-day menu featuring:
- Korean fried chicken wings
- Sesame marinated cucumbers
- Fried shrimp with pickled red onion and lemon mayonnaise
- Grilled skewers
- **Kimbap section**: Bluefin tuna with cucumber/avocado, fried chicken with pickled chiles, Australian wagyu beef
- **Main courses**: Seared king salmon, mushroom lo mein, roasted half chicken, slow-cooked baby back ribs, lobster noodles, steaks

## Critical Reception

The Infatuation's review: "Super Peach is a good restaurant, but a better mall restaurant" — suggesting the concept excels in its specific context (mall dining) even if it doesn't reach the heights of Chang's earlier fine-dining work.

## Strategic Significance

Super Peach represents the new Momofuku restaurant model:
1. **No Michelin aspirations**: Accessible, all-day dining rather than tasting menu fine dining
2. **LA market**: Chang's personal base (he films Dinner Time Live in downtown LA); LA expansion makes personal and business sense
3. **Mall placement**: Premium captive audience, guaranteed foot traffic, validates Chang's own 2018 prediction about restaurants as mall anchors
4. **Brand extension**: Restaurant serves as physical brand touchpoint for the Momofuku Goods products used in-house

## CNBC 2018 Quote Becomes 2025 Reality

Chang told CNBC in February 2018: "Restaurants are replacing retail as anchor tenants, and 'everyone wants one.'" Super Peach at Westfield Century City is the literal execution of this prediction seven years later.
