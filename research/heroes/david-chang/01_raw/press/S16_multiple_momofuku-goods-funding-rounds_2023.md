## Overview

In 2023, Momofuku Goods raised two separate rounds of growth capital totaling approximately $27.5–$29 million, establishing it as a serious consumer packaged goods business backed by institutional investors with deep CPG expertise.

## Round 1: Series A — $17.5 Million (March 2023)

**Amount**: $17.5 million
**Lead investor**: Siddhi Capital
**Context**: Siddhi Capital is a CPG-focused venture firm with a portfolio of food and beverage brands. Its involvement signals institutional validation of the Momofuku Goods business.

Retail Dive's coverage of the raise noted the company's plans to use capital for:
- Retail distribution expansion
- Marketing and brand building
- Product development
- Team building for the CPG division

**Retail Dive analysis**: The raise positioned Momofuku Goods as a serious competitor in the specialty condiments and pantry ingredients space — not just a restaurant brand licensing its name to packaged products.

## Round 2: $11.5 Million (Late 2023)

**Amount**: $11.5 million
**Lead investor**: Alliance Consumer Growth (ACG)
**Context**: ACG is a consumer-focused growth equity firm. Its portfolio includes other premium food brands.

NOSH (a CPG-focused trade publication) covered the second raise as evidence of Momofuku Goods' momentum — two rounds in the same year is unusual and reflects strong early performance metrics.

**PR Newswire press release headline**: "Momofuku Goods Announces New Growth Funding from Alliance Consumer Growth"

## Combined 2023 Fundraising

Total raised in 2023: approximately $27.5–$29 million
Total raised (all rounds): approximately $30 million+

## Business Metrics at Time of Raises

- Revenue at time of Series A: not disclosed but growing rapidly
- Retail locations at end of 2023: 6,600
- Revenue at end of 2023: $50 million
- Revenue at end of 2024: $67 million+

## The Spoon Coverage

The Spoon — a food technology trade publication — framed the raise as validation of David Chang's "pantry essentials" vision:
- Chang's thesis: The gap between restaurant-quality flavors and what's available at grocery store level is massive
- Momofuku Goods' mission: Close that gap with restaurant-grade condiments and pantry staples at consumer price points
- The raise enabled the retail infrastructure to execute on this vision at scale

## Investor Thesis

Both Siddhi Capital and Alliance Consumer Growth are betting on:
1. **Brand premium**: Momofuku's 20-year brand equity commands retail shelf space and consumer trust
2. **Asian food category growth**: Explosive growth in Asian-inspired condiments (chili crisp, soy sauce, etc.) in US grocery market
3. **Celebrity chef CPG playbook**: Chang as the proven proof-of-concept for the restaurant-to-CPG transition
4. **Gross margin opportunity**: At $67M revenue with growing retail scale, CPG margins are significantly better than restaurant margins

## Significance

The double raise in 2023 enabled Momofuku to:
1. Expand from ~3,000 to 6,600+ retail locations in one year
2. Invest in marketing during the chili crunch controversy (which, counterintuitively, drove awareness)
3. Bring in veteran CPG leadership (Padraic "Paddy" Spence as Goods CEO in 2025)
4. Build toward the $100M combined revenue threshold
