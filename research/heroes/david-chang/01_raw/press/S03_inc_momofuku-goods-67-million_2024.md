## Overview

Momofuku Goods grew almost 585 percent between 2021 and 2024 in the global flavors space — a category shaped by social media virality and the growing popularity of Asian flavors in American cooking.

## Revenue Growth

- End of 2023: $50 million in annual revenue
- End of 2024: Past $67 million in annual revenue
- Growth rate: ~585% between 2021–2024
- Retail presence by end of 2023: 6,600 retail locations
- Retail presence by December 2024: 10,300 retail locations

## Retail Partners

Products now available at: Target, Kroger, Costco, Wegmans, Sprouts, Whole Foods, and thousands of independents.

For the first time in December 2024, brick-and-mortar sales outpaced direct-to-consumer revenue.

## Funding History

- March 2023: $17.5 million Series A from Siddhi Capital

## Origin and Strategy

From the very beginning, the goal wasn't to package the restaurant dishes, but to ensure that Momofuku's building blocks were accessible at home. Chang identified "a massive gap between the flavors being made in the restaurants and what was available at the grocery store."

The company's breakthrough came when a buyer for Whole Foods Market sampled Momofuku's Chili Crunch and wanted to stock it across the Northeast — this catalyzed the retail expansion.

## Business Transformation

The Goods division now accounts for over half of Momofuku's total revenue. For the first time, Momofuku will make as much money from its non-restaurant ventures as it does from its restaurants, for a combined $100 million total.

## Product Strategy

Rather than replicating restaurant dishes, Momofuku Goods focused on pantry staples and building-block ingredients:
- Chili Crunch (the flagship breakthrough product)
- Seasoned salts
- Air-dried noodles
- Soy sauce
- Chocolate bars
- Other pantry goods

## QR Code / Digital Data Strategy

Momofuku Goods began heavily investing in brand growth in 2024, creating personalized digital experiences via QR codes on packaging. This allowed the company to collect customer data across different sales channels — bridging physical retail and digital engagement — while curating seasonal recipe content, promoting new product launches, and adjusting the overall consumer experience with ease.

## Leadership

In February 2025, the company brought in veteran CPG executive Padraic "Paddy" Spence as CEO of the Goods business to accelerate expansion.

## CEO Marguerite Zabar Mariscal

Since she became CEO of the Momofuku parent company in 2019, Mariscal engineered a deliberate shift: closing underperforming restaurant concepts while aggressively building the consumer packaged goods business. This pivot has been credited with transforming Momofuku from a restaurant group into a diversified food and media company.

## Chang's Quote on Accessibility

"We want Momofuku products to feel as natural on your counter as salt and pepper."
