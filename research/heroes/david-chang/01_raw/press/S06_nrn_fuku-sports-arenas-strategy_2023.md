## Overview

Fuku — David Chang's fast-casual fried chicken sandwich concept — pivoted away from standalone restaurant locations toward a concessions-only model focused on sports stadiums and arenas. This strategic decision has driven rapid unit growth.

## The Fuku Origin Story

Fuku started as a secret off-menu sandwich at Momofuku Noodle Bar in NYC. It became an actual restaurant in its own right before evolving into its current concessions-focused model.

- 2015: Fuku launched as a standalone fast-casual concept
- 2020: Closed some locations during pandemic
- 2021: Pivoted to ghost kitchen/delivery model via Reef Technologies partnership
- 2022–2023: Aggressively expanded into sports venue concessions
- 2023: Named Alex Muñoz-Suarez as first-ever CEO of Fuku

## Stadium and Concession Strategy

Fuku has landed on a model that's led to fast growth: sports stadiums and arenas as the primary channel.

**Current locations include:**
- Madison Square Garden, New York
- Yankee Stadium, New York (partnership with Legends Hospitality, announced March 2023)
- Citi Field, New York
- Hard Rock Stadium, Miami
- T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas
- Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas
- TIAA Bank Field (Jacksonville Jaguars)
- FedEx Field (Washington Commanders)
- Approximately 30 total concession locations across the US

**Financial target:** Fuku leadership has stated a goal of reaching $100 million in annual system-wide sales within five years.

## Why Arenas?

The sports venue model solves several key operational challenges that plagued standalone restaurant operations:
1. **Captive audience**: Stadium attendees have limited food options
2. **No real estate overhead**: Concession agreements vs. long-term leases
3. **Managed traffic spikes**: Known event schedules allow preparation
4. **Marketing exposure**: Association with high-profile sports brands
5. **Partnership leverage**: Works with major foodservice operators (Legends Hospitality, Delaware North, Levy Restaurants, Sodexo)

## Leadership Structure

The appointment of Alex Muñoz-Suarez as Fuku's first CEO — previously president of Momofuku — signals the brand's intention to operate independently of the broader Momofuku restaurant group and pursue a standalone growth trajectory.

## The Menu

The concept's iconic Spicy Fried Chicken Sando is on the menu, along with chicken tenders and waffle fries. The menu is deliberately tight and scalable — ideal for high-volume concession environments.

## Super Bowl Presence

In 2024, Fuku brought its menu to Super Bowl 58 in Las Vegas — a high-profile moment underscoring the brand's association with major sports events and its ambition to be the go-to celebrity chef brand in sports concessions.

## Miami Expansion

Fuku opened in Coral Gables on Miracle Mile in 2026 as the first of two South Florida locations (a second was planned for West Palm Beach). This expansion represents a rare move back into standalone retail locations alongside the concessions strategy.
