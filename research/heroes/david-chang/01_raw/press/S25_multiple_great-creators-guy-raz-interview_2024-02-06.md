## Overview

David Chang appeared on The Great Creators with Guy Raz podcast on February 6, 2024 — a longform interview focused on his creative process, entrepreneurial evolution, and how Momofuku grew from a single 600-square-foot location to a multi-format food and media empire.

## Key Themes from the Interview

### Creative Drive
The episode title — "I'm Addicted to Creative Excitement" — captures Chang's self-diagnosis as someone driven by novelty and creative challenge rather than the maintenance of existing success. This explains the serial pivots: noodle bar → fine dining → magazine → media company → CPG → live Netflix.

### Momofuku's Growth Arc
Chang discussed how Momofuku grew from:
- A single 600-square-foot noodle bar (2004)
- To a Michelin-starred restaurant (2009)
- To a bakery and dessert chain (Milk Bar)
- To a line of consumer packaged goods (Momofuku Goods, $67M in 2024)

### The CPG Transition
Chang acknowledged that he started to grow his CPG business and now spends less time on the restaurant side — a rare direct statement from Chang about the deliberate deprioritization of restaurants in the company strategy.

### The Only Problem with Grocery

Chang noted: "The only problem with consumer product goods and retail and grocery stores is that it moves at a snail's pace" — a candid acknowledgment that the CPG business requires patience that restaurant operators (accustomed to daily feedback loops) sometimes struggle with.

### Fatherhood and Purpose

The Guy Raz podcast context (The Great Creators focuses on founding stories) allowed Chang to discuss how becoming a father shifted his sense of purpose. His son Hugo (born 2018) appears as a recurring theme in Chang's post-2018 media work — fatherhood seems to have moderated the intensity that characterized his early career.
