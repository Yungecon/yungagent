## Article Summaries

**S01 — Wikipedia: David Chang Career Overview**
Comprehensive timeline of Chang's career from 2004 founding of Momofuku Noodle Bar through the 2025 opening of Super Peach. Covers all restaurant openings, closures, media projects, awards, and personal milestones with dates and financial context.

**S02 — 25iq: Business Lessons from David Chang**
Analysis of Chang's bootstrapped business model — using capital constraints to force creative decisions, building a multi-format restaurant portfolio at different price points, and using media as brand distribution. Covers the lean startup logic behind Momofuku's early growth.

**S03 — Inc. Magazine: Momofuku Goods $67M Powerhouse**
The most detailed available account of Momofuku Goods' consumer packaged goods growth story: $50M by end of 2023, $67M by end of 2024, 585% growth 2021–2024, 10,300+ retail locations. Covers funding rounds, the Whole Foods breakthrough, the QR code data strategy, and leadership changes.

**S04 — Fast Company: Momofuku CEO and $100M Empire**
Profile of CEO Marguerite Zabar Mariscal's strategic transformation of Momofuku — from restaurant empire to diversified $100M food and media business. Covers the deliberate restaurant closure strategy, CPG pivot logic, and the combined revenue milestone.

**S05 — BuzzFeed/Yahoo: Chili Crunch Trademark Crisis**
Detailed account of the 2024 chili crunch trademark controversy: Momofuku's cease-and-desist campaign against AAPI-owned businesses, the "culinary colonizer" community backlash, boycott calls, and Chang's April 12 apology on his podcast dropping enforcement of the trademark.

**S06 — Nation's Restaurant News: Fuku Sports Arena Strategy**
Deep dive into Fuku's pivot to sports venue concessions — 30+ locations at MSG, Yankee Stadium, Hard Rock Stadium, T-Mobile Arena, Allegiant Stadium, and others; working with Legends Hospitality, Delaware North, Levy, and Sodexo; targeting $100M system-wide sales.

**S07 — Hollywood Reporter: Majordomo Media Launch**
Coverage of Chang's March 2018 announcement of Majordomo Media — his own production company (co-founded with Christopher Chen and Scott Dadich) to produce podcasts, TV, and editorial content. The foundational document for understanding Chang as a media operator.

**S08 — Netflix/Multiple: Ugly Delicious Media Strategy**
Analysis of Ugly Delicious (2018–2020) as a format innovation — food as cultural and political commentary rather than cooking instruction; the NBC News analysis of why it succeeds; Emmy nomination; global Netflix distribution as Momofuku marketing; Season 2's shift to more personal storytelling.

**S09 — Variety: Dinner Time Live Netflix Renewal**
Coverage of Netflix renewing Dinner Time Live for Season 2 (and subsequent Season 3 in August 2025); analysis of Chang's unique position as a live programming anchor for Netflix; the business logic of live culinary content vs. produced food docs.

**S10 — Food52/Multiple: Lucky Peach Magazine**
History of Lucky Peach (2011–2017): founding with Peter Meehan, Chris Ying, and Zero Point Zero Productions; 24 quarterly volumes; iconoclastic editorial voice; significance as Chang's first owned media property; the closure and lessons that informed the Majordomo Media pivot to video and audio.

**S11 — Awful Announcing/Spotify/Deadline: The Dave Chang Show Podcast**
Coverage of the podcast's history on The Ringer, multiyear deal renewal, Recipe Club spinoff with Majordomo Media, and the 2025 Netflix/Ringer/Spotify deal that migrated The Dave Chang Show into Netflix's podcast slate — 906+ episodes as of 2026.

**S12 — Lo Times/Robb Report/Restaurant Hospitality: Restaurant Closures 2020–2023**
Comprehensive account of the deliberate closure of Momofuku's landmark restaurant concepts: Nishi and CCDC (2020), Ssäm Bar (September 2023), Ko (November 2023). Frames closures as strategic pivot to CPG rather than collapse, with CEO Mariscal's strategy as the through-line.

**S13 — NPR: Eat a Peach Memoir and Mental Health**
David Chang's 2020 memoir as a vehicle for discussing bipolar disorder, depression, affective dysregulation, and how the Momofuku project functioned as both business and mental health structure. The "textbook on what not to do when starting a business" framing; fatherhood as a transformative factor.

**S14 — Boston.com/Multiple: Fuku Ghost Kitchen Pandemic Pivot**
The 2020 pandemic pivot of Fuku from standalone restaurants to ghost kitchen model via Reef Technologies partnership, with delivery-only expansion to Seattle, Dallas, Baltimore, and other cities — a distribution experiment that proved demand before the stadium concessions pivot.

**S15 — BusinessWire: Fuku-Yankees-Legends Hospitality Partnership**
The March 22, 2023 press release announcing Fuku's partnership with the New York Yankees and Legends Hospitality, placing Fuku in Yankee Stadium — a landmark deal establishing Fuku's premium sports association and opening the door to the Legends stadium network.

**S16 — Retail Dive/NOSH/Multiple: Momofuku Goods 2023 Funding Rounds**
Coverage of the two 2023 funding rounds ($17.5M Series A from Siddhi Capital; $11.5M from Alliance Consumer Growth) totaling approximately $27.5–$29M, enabling aggressive retail expansion from ~3,000 to 6,600+ locations and positioning the company for the $50M year-end revenue milestone.

**S17 — Hollywood Reporter/Multiple: Super Peach Century City 2025**
Coverage of the October 2025 opening of Super Peach by Momofuku at Westfield Century City, Los Angeles — Chang's return to full-service restaurant operation after the 2023 closures, positioned as a mall anchor in a premium LA location, validating Chang's own 2018 CNBC prediction about restaurants replacing retail as anchor tenants.

**S18 — Fast Company: 20 Years of Momofuku's Impact**
20th anniversary retrospective of how Momofuku Noodle Bar changed American dining: democratizing fine dining techniques, mainstreaming Asian ingredients, building a media empire alongside restaurants, pioneering the celebrity chef CPG pivot, and changing food television from instruction to cultural commentary.

**S19 — James Beard Foundation: Who's Who Induction 2014**
James Beard Foundation's 2014 Who's Who induction profile of Chang (at age 36 — exceptionally young), covering his six James Beard Awards, his philosophy of bridging fine cooking and everyday eating, and his positioning alongside Julia Child, Alice Waters, and Wolfgang Puck in the culinary canon.

**S20 — Parade/Multiple: Net Worth and Revenue Breakdown**
Aggregated financial picture of Chang's income streams: Momofuku equity (restaurants + Goods), Netflix deals ($1–3M/year estimated), podcast revenue, speaking fees, book royalties, brand partnerships. Net worth estimates range $20M–$100M depending on equity valuation methodology.

**S21 — HuffPost/Salon/Taste Cooking: Korean-American Identity**
Chang's ongoing engagement with questions of Korean-American identity, cultural authenticity, and what it means to cook "American food" as an Asian-American; how this identity dimension functions as competitive advantage for his brand; the Kimchi Revolution in American food culture; why the chili crunch trademark controversy was so damaging given this brand foundation.

**S22 — Wikipedia/eGullet: The Mind of a Chef (PBS)**
Coverage of the PBS documentary series that established Chang as an intellectual food figure (Season 1, 2012), narrated and executive produced by Anthony Bourdain; the Bourdain template Chang followed from restaurant → PBS → memoir; the PBS format as prestige distribution for the educated affluent Momofuku demographic.

**S23 — Consumer Goods Technology: Momofuku Goods QR Code Data Strategy**
2024 initiative using QR codes on Momofuku Goods packaging to bridge the retail data gap — building first-party consumer data from brick-and-mortar retail purchases, enabling personalized content delivery, CRM list building, and retention marketing for the 10,300+ retail location customer base.

**S24 — Salon/QSR Magazine: Chili Crunch Trademark Deep Dive**
Comprehensive explainer on the trademark controversy — the legal strategy, the IP vs. generic term question, why the AAPI community reaction was so intense (Lao Gan Ma historical context), the power asymmetry against small AAPI founders, trade publication analysis of reputational vs. business risk, and lessons for brand management.

**S25 — The Great Creators with Guy Raz: 2024 Interview**
Chang on The Great Creators podcast discussing his "addiction to creative excitement" as the driver of serial pivots; Momofuku's growth from a 600 sq ft noodle bar to a $100M+ business; the CPG transition and its challenge (grocery "moves at a snail's pace"); fatherhood as a reframing of success; Chang's positioning as a CPG executive speaking to institutional investors.
