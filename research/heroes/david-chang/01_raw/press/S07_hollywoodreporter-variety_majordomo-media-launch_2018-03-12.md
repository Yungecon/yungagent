## Overview

On March 12, 2018, David Chang announced the launch of Majordomo Media — a full-stack media company intended to serve as an umbrella for podcasts, television development, editorial content, and digital video.

## Company Structure

**Majordomo Media** was founded by:
- David Chang (founder, Momofuku)
- Christopher Chen (former business development executive at Looper producer Endgame Entertainment)
- Scott Dadich (former Wired editor-in-chief; his firm Godfrey Dadich Partners brought design and strategy)
- Lou Cona (former Condé Nast Media Group president) as Chief of Business, Strategy, and Revenue

Named after Chang's Los Angeles restaurant of the same name.

## The Vision

"Majordomo Media is a place for people to connect over the things I believe define culture: travel, food, music, sports, exploration," Chang said.

The platform was designed to teach people "how to find — and understand — new and different things themselves and let them bring their friends along for the ride."

## Content Slate

Initial content plans included:
- Podcast series (The Dave Chang Show launched on The Ringer)
- Several television shows in development
- Editorial and digital media content

## Strategic Significance

Majordomo Media represented Chang's deliberate move from being a chef who appeared on TV to being a media operator who owned distribution. The company became the production entity behind his Netflix deals (Ugly Delicious, Breakfast Lunch & Dinner, The Next Thing You Eat, Dinner Time Live) and the Ringer podcast partnership.

## Partnership with The Ringer

The Dave Chang Show launched on The Ringer podcast network (Bill Simmons' company, acquired by Spotify). The Dave Chang Show was renewed for a multiyear deal. In 2020, The Ringer and Majordomo Media also launched Recipe Club — a podcast where Chang and Chris Ying, plus a rotating featured guest, cook and critique different recipes.

## Netflix Relationship

The Ugly Delicious deal was a landmark:
- Season 1 (2018): 8 episodes exploring the politics and culture of food globally
- Season 2 (2020): 4 episodes, more personal, Emmy-nominated
- Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner (2019): City-by-city exploration with celebrity guests
- The Next Thing You Eat (2021): Hulu (future of food)
- Dinner Time Live (2024–present): Netflix's live programming; Season 1 January 2024; Season 2 October 2024 (holiday-themed); Season 3 August 2025

## Netflix Live Programming

Dinner Time Live is part of Netflix's push into live programming. Chang hosts celebrity guests in his downtown Los Angeles kitchen, cooking without food stylists or swap-outs — raw, live, with mishaps shown in real time. Season 1 guests included Aziz Ansari, Lily Collins, Terry Crews, Gabriel Iglesias, Lil Jon, Rashida Jones, Seth Rogen, Maya Rudolph, and Steven Yeun. Netflix renewed the show for Season 2 before the end of the first season.

## Dave Chang Show → Netflix Podcast Migration

The Dave Chang Show was included in Netflix's initial podcast slate launch with The Ringer and Spotify, a deal that moved full episodes from YouTube to the Netflix/Spotify ecosystem.

The company model — original talent → production company → distribution deals → brand licensing → product sales — is a template for hospitality figures building durable, multi-channel businesses.
