## Overview

The Dave Chang Show is David Chang's flagship podcast, launched in 2018 on The Ringer podcast network (Bill Simmons' company, acquired by Spotify). As of 2026, it has produced over 906 episodes and releases new episodes every Monday and Thursday.

## Format

Chang (and co-host Chris Ying) engages in wide-ranging conversations with guests covering:
- The creative process
- Failures and successes
- Food and culture
- Identity and fame
- Sports, music, and broader cultural topics

The podcast is deliberately not a cooking show — it is a culture/interview show where food is one lens among many.

## Partnership Structure

**The Ringer**: Chang's show sits within the Ringer podcast network alongside sports and culture shows. The Dave Chang Show was renewed for a multiyear deal.

**Majordomo Media**: Chang's own production company (launched 2018) co-produces the podcast, giving him ownership stakes in the content.

**Recipe Club**: A separate Ringer/Majordomo podcast launched November 12, 2020, where Chang, Ying, and a rotating featured guest all cook the same dish and provide unfiltered critiques.

## The Netflix/Ringer/Spotify Deal (2025)

In late 2025, Netflix announced an initial podcast slate launch with The Ringer and Spotify. The Dave Chang Show was included in this slate — a deal that moved full episodes from YouTube to the Netflix/Spotify ecosystem, restricting YouTube to clips only.

This deal positioned The Dave Chang Show as part of Netflix's audio content ambitions, creating additional distribution for Chang within the Netflix ecosystem (alongside Dinner Time Live).

Deadline's analysis noted the deal "skips comedy," focusing on sports, culture, and food — genres where The Ringer (and Chang's show) have existing audiences.

## Business Model / Revenue Streams for Chang

The podcast represents:
1. **License/distribution fees**: From The Ringer/Spotify deal
2. **Advertising revenue**: Podcast host-read ads (before/during streaming deal)
3. **Brand extension**: Show extends Momofuku brand into audio
4. **Content IP**: Episodes can be repurposed across platforms
5. **Guest relationships**: Show provides access to celebrities, athletes, chefs — feeding into other Momofuku and Majordomo projects

## Scale

906+ episodes (as of 2026), released biweekly. Available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube (clips), and all major podcast platforms.

## Significance for Hero Study

The Dave Chang Show demonstrates Chang's mastery of long-form audio as a relationship-building and distribution tool. The show's wide cultural scope (beyond food) allowed Chang to build an audience that transcends the traditional "foodie" demographic — reaching sports fans, culture enthusiasts, and business thinkers. This breadth of audience has value for the Momofuku Goods CPG business, which needs to reach mainstream consumer households, not just restaurant-goers.
