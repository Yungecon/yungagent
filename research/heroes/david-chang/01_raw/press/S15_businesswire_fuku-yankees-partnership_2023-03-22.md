## Overview

On March 22, 2023, Fuku — David Chang's fried chicken sandwich brand — announced a partnership with the New York Yankees and Legends Hospitality, placing Fuku in Yankee Stadium for the 2023 baseball season. This partnership represents a milestone in Fuku's concessions strategy — landing in one of the highest-profile sports venues in the world.

## The Partnership

**Partners:**
- Fuku (Momofuku subsidiary, fast-casual fried chicken brand)
- New York Yankees (MLB team, one of the most valuable sports franchises globally)
- Legends Hospitality (premium experiences and venue services company; Yankees co-owned)

**Location:** Yankee Stadium, The Bronx, New York

**Launch:** Before the 2023 baseball season

## Strategic Significance

The Yankees partnership is strategically important for several reasons:

1. **Brand prestige**: The Yankees are one of the most recognized sports brands globally — association elevates Fuku's brand perception
2. **High-volume venue**: Yankee Stadium hosts 81 home games/year plus concerts and other events
3. **Legends Hospitality relationship**: Legends operates premium hospitality at numerous major venues; this relationship opened doors to other Legends-managed stadiums
4. **NYC market validation**: Fuku returning to NYC's high-profile food scene via a sports venue, after closing some of its standalone NYC restaurant locations

## Fuku's Stadium Portfolio (by mid-2023)

Following the Yankees partnership, Fuku's concession locations included:
- Yankee Stadium (MLB)
- Citi Field (MLB, New York Mets)
- Madison Square Garden (NBA/NHL, New York Knicks/Rangers)
- Hard Rock Stadium (NFL, Miami Dolphins)
- T-Mobile Arena (NHL, Vegas Golden Knights)
- Allegiant Stadium (NFL, Las Vegas Raiders)
- TIAA Bank Field (NFL, Jacksonville Jaguars)
- FedEx Field (NFL, Washington Commanders)
- Approximately 30 locations total

## Working with Foodservice Operators

Fuku's stadium strategy relies on partnerships with the major foodservice management companies that control concession rights at large venues:
- **Legends Hospitality** (Yankees, others)
- **Delaware North** (multiple venues)
- **Levy Restaurants** (multiple venues)
- **Sodexo** (multiple venues)

This means Fuku doesn't need to negotiate venue-by-venue — a single relationship with a major operator can open dozens of venues simultaneously.

## Financial Target

Fuku leadership stated a goal of reaching **$100 million in annual system-wide sales within five years** of the stadium expansion push.

## CEO Appointment

Coinciding with the stadium expansion, Fuku named its first-ever CEO: **Alex Muñoz-Suarez**, formerly president of Momofuku. The appointment signals Fuku's intent to operate with increasing independence from the broader Momofuku group.

## The Fried Chicken Sandwich Market Context

The Fuku expansion happened against the backdrop of the "chicken sandwich wars" — a period of intense competition in the QSR fried chicken category led by Chick-fil-A, Popeyes, McDonald's, and others. Fuku's differentiation: celebrity chef provenance, premium positioning, and unique venue selection (stadiums rather than traditional QSR strip mall locations).
