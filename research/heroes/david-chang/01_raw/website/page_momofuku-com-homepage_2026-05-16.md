### Brand story context
- Founded 2004 with Momofuku Noodle Bar, East Village, NYC
- Named after Momofuku Ando (inventor of instant ramen) — both homage and irony given Chang's own instant noodle products
- Six James Beard Awards including Outstanding Chef
- Expanded from fine dining (Ko, 2 Michelin stars) to fast casual (Fuku, Super Peach) to CPG retail
- The restaurant group and goods brand are parallel but increasingly distinct commercial entities
