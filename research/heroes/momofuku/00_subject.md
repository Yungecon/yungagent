## Who this is

Restaurant group founded by David Chang in 2004. The study is Momofuku-as-platform — how a single ramen bar became a multi-vertical brand spanning restaurants, media, consumer packaged goods (Momofuku Goods), and a digital presence. The brand arc is: room → ritual → language → media → product → platform → power check. This is the canonical YUNG hero study.

## Scope

Full Mode B hero study. Brand focus — the CPG + media expansion arc is the priority over the restaurant operations layer.

**Time window:** 2004–present. Era bucketing:
- `2004-2010_founding` — single location, cult status, pre-media
- `2011-2016_expansion` — restaurant group scaling, Lucky Peach magazine
- `2017-2020_media` — Netflix era, peak brand heat
- `2021-2022_products` — Momofuku Goods CPG launch (noodles, chili crunch)
- `2023-present_platform` — full platform posture, what's compounding now

---

## What we're trying to learn

1. How did Momofuku translate restaurant authority into a CPG brand people buy at Whole Foods?
2. What is the full monetization stack today — restaurants / CPG / media / brand deals?
3. What content strategy drives the CPG side vs. the restaurant side?
4. What is the playbook an operator could steal from the transition from service business to product business?
