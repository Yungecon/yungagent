## Who this is

Business and finance media brand founded by Rashad Bilal and Troy Millings. Started as a podcast, grew into YouTube (2M+ subscribers), Instagram, live events, courses, and a full media company. The structural model is what YUNG studies here — how a podcast becomes a platform, how an audience becomes a business. Not hospitality-native; the translation is: what does the EYL operational model look like if you drop it into food and beverage?

## What we're trying to learn

EYL is the operating model reference. They took a niche audience (Black wealth-building) and built a full-stack media business — free content → paid courses → live events → brand deals → their own fund. That sequencing is exactly what YUNG wants to understand. The hospitality translation: what would EYL look like if the subject was restaurant ownership, not stock portfolios?

1. How did they sequence distribution → monetization?
2. What does the revenue stack look like (ad revenue / courses / events / brand deals / fund)?
3. How did they handle the transition from free content to paid products without losing the audience?
4. What content formats drove the growth that mattered (saves, shares, subscribers)?
