## Who this is

Author of Building a StoryBrand and founder of StoryBrand / Business Made Simple. The StoryBrand framework is a 7-part narrative structure for clarifying brand messaging: Hero → Problem → Guide → Plan → Call to Action → Avoiding Failure → Success. Miller's argument is that confusion kills brands — the clearer your message, the more it travels. Not a content creator in the modern sense; a frameworks reference.
