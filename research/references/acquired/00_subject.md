## Who this is

Business history podcast hosted by Ben Gilbert and David Rosenthal. Each episode is a multi-hour deep dive into the history of one company — how it was built, what decisions mattered, what the architecture looks like now. Acquired is the structural model for YUNG hero studies. The format, the narrative architecture, the ratio of history to insight — all of it is a direct reference point for what YUNG is building with hero study content.

This is the most important reference in the entire library. The Acquired source folder (in Notion) has the Playbook + Principles pages. The Acquired Playbook is the template YUNG hero studies follow.

## What we're trying to learn

Acquired is a format reference, not a distribution reference. YUNG is not trying to build a podcast (yet). What it's stealing is:

1. **Narrative architecture** — how Acquired structures a company history into act-driven storytelling. Era chapters, character maps, turning points.
2. **The ratio** — the Founding Document's "70% history, 15% interpretation, 10% lessons, 5% Yung translation" is directly derived from watching how Acquired earns its takes.
3. **Claim discipline** — how Acquired handles the line between reported fact and interpretation. The Claim Bank's hard-fact / reported / Yung-interpretation taxonomy mirrors Acquired's sourcing discipline.
4. **Segment architecture** — the Acquired episode structure (world before, founding, scaling, empire, lessons) maps onto the war room Segment Packets.
