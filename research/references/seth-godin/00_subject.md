## Who this is

Marketing author and blogger. Permission Marketing, This Is Marketing, The Dip, Purple Cow — 20+ books, one of the most-read daily blogs in marketing. The specific lens YUNG takes from Godin: permission vs. interruption, finding the smallest viable audience, and the concept of a "minimum viable audience" that gives you permission to grow. Not a content creator in the modern sense — a thinker whose frameworks pre-date the creator economy but map onto it almost perfectly.

## What we're trying to learn

Godin is a concepts reference. Not studying his content strategy — studying his ideas. Specific concepts with direct YUNG application:

1. **Permission Marketing** — the operator who opted into your list has given you permission to speak to them. Every email subscriber is permission. Every follower who saves a post is a micro-permission signal.
2. **Smallest viable audience** — YUNG is not for everyone and is not trying to be. Godin's framework for why niching is a strength, not a weakness.
3. **Remarkable = worth remarking about** — the Purple Cow framework applied to hospitality content. What makes something share-worthy vs. just competent?
4. **Linchpin and shipping** — the bias toward publishing over perfecting. Relevant to cadence discipline.
